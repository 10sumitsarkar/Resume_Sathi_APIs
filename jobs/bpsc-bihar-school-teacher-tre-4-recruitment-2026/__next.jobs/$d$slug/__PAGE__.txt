<h2>Selection Process</h2>
<p>Selection under TRE 4.0 works on a single-stage, merit-based model rather than multiple rounds of interviews. Since STET/CTET has already screened candidates for subject and teaching eligibility, BPSC's own test focuses on ranking rather than re-testing basic qualification.</p>
<ul>
<li>Computer Based Test (CBT) - Objective Type Questions Covering General Studies, Language and Subject-Related Areas Depending on the Post</li>
<li>Document Verification for Candidates Who Clear the CBT Cutoff</li>
<li>Final Merit List Prepared Based on CBT Score, District and Category-Wise Preferences</li>
</ul>
