<h2>Rajasthan High Court Stenographer Preparation Tips 2026</h2>
<h3>1. Shorthand (Most Important)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Practice Daily: Start Slow from 40-50 WPM and Gradually Increase. Aim to Increase Speed by 5 WPM Every Week Until You Reach 80+.WPM&nbsp;</li>
<li>Transcription Matters Too: Writing Fast Is Not Enough, You Must Be Able to Transcribe It Back Into Accurate English Without Errors or using Backspace.</li>
<li>Dedicate a Minimum of 1-2 Hours Daily necause typing Built on Muscle Memory so taking a Break Causes Speed to Drop.</li>
</ul>
<h3>2. English (Highest Weightage in the Written Exam)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Grammar: Practice 10-15 Questions Daily on Tenses, Articles, Prepositions and Verb.</li>
<li>Vocabulary: Learn 10 New Words Daily With Their Synonyms and Antonyms.</li>
<li>Comprehension: Read 2-3 Passages a Week and Solve Questions on Them, This Also Improves Reading speed.</li>
</ul>
<h3>3. Reasoning (A Speed Test)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Series, Coding-Decoding, Analogy, Blood Relations (These Are All Pattern Based, So the More You Practice the Faster You Will Solve Them).</li>
<li>Practice With a Timer because Speed Matters in the Real Exam.</li>
</ul>
<h3>4. General Awareness</h3>
<ul>
<li>Current Affairs: Cover the Last 6 Months Current Affairs by Read Newspaper or a Current Affairs in daily basis for at least 30 to 60 Minutes.</li>
<li>Static GK: Revise History, Polity and Geography a Little Every Day.</li>
</ul>
