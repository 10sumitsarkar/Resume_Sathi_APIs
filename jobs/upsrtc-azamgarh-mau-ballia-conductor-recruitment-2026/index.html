UPSRTC Azamgarh, Mau, Ballia Bus Conductor Recruitment 2026

24 Jul 2026Company
Apply From20 Jul 2026
Last Date05 Aug 2026

Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (UPSRTC) has released the advertisement for Bus Conductor Outsourcing Recruitment 2026 for Azamgarh, Mau and Ballia districts. Candidates interested and eligible from these districts can apply online through the Sewayojan Portal from 20 July 2026 to 05 August 2026.

Important Dates

Application Begin 20/07/2026
Last Date for Apply 05/08/2026
Interview Date As per Schedule

Application Fee

  • General / OBC / EWS : 0/-
  • SC / ST : 0/-
  • No Application Fee for Any Candidate

Age Limit

  • Minimum Age : 18 Years
  • Maximum Age : 45 Years
  • Age Relaxation Extra as per UPSRTC Conductor Outsourcing Recruitment Rules

Vacancy Details Total : 35 Post

District Name Total Post UPSRTC Conductor Eligibility
Azamgarh, Mau, Ballia 35
  • Passed Class 12th (Intermediate) From Any Recognized Board in India
  • Candidate Must Be Medically Fit
  • No Prior Experience Required
  • CCC Exam Passed

Selection Process

  • Registration and Profile Update on the Sewayojan Portal (Rojgar Sangam)
  • Document Verification
  • Interview - As per Schedule

How to Fill UPSRTC Conductor Outsourcing Online Form 2026

  • Register on the Sewayojan Portal if Not Already Registered, and Update Your Qualification and CCC Certificate Details in Your Profile.
  • Once the Profile Is Updated, the Option to Apply for UPSRTC Conductor Recruitment Will Appear on the Portal.

Note: Candidates are advised to always cross-check dates, eligibility criteria, and the application process from the official UPSRTC and Sewayojan Portal before applying, as the schedule may change based on official updates.

UPSRTC Conductor Preparation Tips 2026

  • Selection Is Through Document Verification and Interview, Keep Your Class 12th Marksheet, CCC Certificate and ID Proof Ready.
  • Prepare Basic Questions About Traffic Rules, Ticketing and Passenger Handling for the Interview Round.
  • Stay Medically Fit, Since Medical Fitness Is a Mandatory Eligibility Requirement for This Post.
  • Carry All Original Documents Along With Photocopies on the Day of Document Verification and Interview.
