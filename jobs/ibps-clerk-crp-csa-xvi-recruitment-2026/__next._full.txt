<h2>Selection Process</h2>
<p>There's no interview at any stage of this recruitment - selection runs purely on your Prelims and Mains scores, followed by document verification. One thing that trips up a lot of first-time applicants: your Prelims marks don't carry forward into the final merit list. Prelims only decides who gets shortlisted for Mains; the Mains score alone determines your final rank.</p>
<ul>
<li>Preliminary Examination - 100 Questions / 100 Marks / 60 Minutes, Split Into English (30Q), Numerical Ability (35Q) and Reasoning Ability (35Q), Each With a 20-Minute Sectional Time Limit</li>
<li>Main Examination - 160 Questions / 200 Marks / 125 Minutes, Covering General/Financial Awareness (40Q/50M), General English (40Q/40M), Reasoning Ability (40Q/60M) and Quantitative Aptitude (40Q/50M)</li>
<li>Document Verification for Candidates Provisionally Allotted a Bank</li>
</ul>
