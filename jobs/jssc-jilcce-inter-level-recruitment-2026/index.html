JSSC JILCCE Inter Level Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 326 Posts

20 Jul 2026Company
Apply From20 Jul 2026
Last Date19 Aug 2026

Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) has released the notification for JILCCE Inter Level Combined Recruitment 2026 for 326 posts. Candidates interested in this Jharkhand JSSC JILCCE Inter Level Recruitment can apply online from 20 July 2026 to 19 August 2026.

Last Date for Apply Online 19/08/2026
Pay Exam Fee Last Date 19/08/2026
Correction Date 21-23 August 2026
JSSC JILCCE Exam Date As per Schedule
Admit Card Available Before Exam

Application Fee

  • General / OBC / Other State : 100/-
  • SC / ST : 50/-
  • Pay the Exam Fee Through Online or Offline Fee Mode Only

Age Limit

  • Minimum Age : 18 Years
  • Maximum Age : 35 Years
  • Age Relaxation Extra as per JSSC JILCCE Inter Level Examination Rules Advt. No. 03/2026

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for JSSC JILCCE Inter Level Recruitment 2026?

The last date to apply online is 19 August 2026, and the fee payment deadline is also 19 August 2026.

How many total vacancies are there in JSSC JILCCE Inter Level Recruitment 2026?

A total of 326 posts have been announced across Health Supervisor, Junior Regional Investigator, Multipurpose Health Worker, Field Assistant/Medical Assistant, Insect Collector and Fisheries Technical Assistant categories.

What is the application fee for this recruitment?

General, OBC and Other State candidates need to pay Rs. 100/-, while SC and ST candidates pay Rs. 50/-.

What is the age limit for JSSC JILCCE Inter Level 2026?

Candidates must be between 18 and 35 years of age, with age relaxation applicable as per JSSC rules.

What is the minimum qualification required for JILCCE Inter Level posts?

Candidates need to have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) from any recognized board in India.

When is the correction window for this recruitment?

The correction window is open from 21 to 23 August 2026 for candidates who need to make changes to their submitted application.

Which department has the highest number of vacancies in this recruitment?

The Directorate of Health Services has the highest number of vacancies through the Health Supervisor post, with 137 openings.
