<h3>1. Prelims - General Knowledge</h3>
<ul>
<li>Focus on West Bengal Specific GK like State History, Geography, Culture and Current Affairs.</li>
<li>Cover National Current Affairs of the Last 6 Months.</li>
<li>Read Newspaper or Current Affairs for 15 to 20 Minutes Daily and try to Make Short Notes for Quick Revision.</li>

<h3>2. Prelims - Arithmetic</h3>
<ul>
<li>Practice Basic Topics Daily: Percentage, Ratio, Average, Profit &amp; Loss, Simple/Compound Interest and Time &amp; Work.</li>
<li>Since the Paper Is Objective With a 60-Minute Time Limit for 100 Questions, Speed and Accuracy Both Matter - Practice With a Timer.</li>
<li>Solve 20-25 Questions Daily, Covering Mixed Topics From Previous Year Papers.</li>
</ul>
<h3>3. Prelims - Reasoning</h3>
<ul>
<li>Practice Series, Coding-Decoding, Analogy, Blood Relations, Direction Sense and Puzzles for Regular basis to Improves Speed.</li>
</ul>
<h3>4. Mains - Descriptive Paper</h3>
<ul>
<li>The Mains Paper Is 400 Marks With 90 Minutes Per Paper so Practice Writing Answers Within the Time Limit.</li>
<li>Read About ICDS Scheme, Women &amp; Child Development Policies and Social Welfare Schemes of West Bengal because These Are Directly Related to the Post.</li>
<li>Practice Bengali Language Sections Regularly because Proficiency in Bengali Is Generally Required for the Supervisor Post.</li>
</ul>
<h3>5. Viva-Voce (Personality Test)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Ready to Answer Questions on the ICDS Scheme, Your Educational Background and Why You Want This Role.</li>
<li>Stay Updated on Current Affairs Related to Women &amp; Child Development.</li>
<li>Practice Mock Interviews to Build Confidence and Work on Clear Communication.</li>
<li>Attempt at Least One Full Mock Test Weekly toTrack Progress.</li>
