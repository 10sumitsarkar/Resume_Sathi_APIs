RRC Prayagraj Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026 (1853 Post)

22 Jul 2026Company
Apply From08 Jul 2026
Last Date07 Aug 2026

North Central Railway (NCR) has released the notification for Various Trade Apprentices Recruitment 2026 under Advt. No. RRC/NCR/Act. Apprentice 01/2026, for 1853 posts. Candidates interested in this Railway RRC Prayagraj Apprentice Recruitment can apply online from 08 July 2026 to 07 August 2026.

Important Dates

Application Begin 08/07/2026
Last Date for Apply Online 07/08/2026
Last Date Pay Exam Fee 07/08/2026
Merit List As per Schedule

Application Fee

  • General / OBC / EWS : 100/-
  • SC / ST : 0/-
  • All Category Female : 0/-
  • Pay the Exam Fee Through Debit Card, Credit Card or Net Banking Mode Only

Age Limit as on 07/07/2026

  • Minimum Age : 15 Years
  • Maximum Age : 24 Years
  • Age Relaxation Extra as per Railway NCR Prayagraj Apprentice Recruitment Rules

Vacancy Details Total : 1853 Post

Post Name Total Post Railway RRC Prayagraj Apprentice Eligibility
Various Trade Apprentice 1853 Class 10 High School Exam With Minimum 50% Marks and ITI / NCVT Certificate in Related Trade

Trade / Division Wise Vacancy Details Are Given in the Official Notification.

Selection Process

  • Merit List Based on Class 10 and ITI Marks
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination (if Applicable as per Notification)

How to Fill Railway RRC NCR Prayagraj Apprentice Online Form 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for RRC Prayagraj Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

The last date to apply online is 07 August 2026.

How many total vacancies are there in this apprentice recruitment?

A total of 1853 posts have been announced for Various Trade Apprentice positions.

Is there an application fee for SC/ST and female candidates?

No, SC/ST candidates and all category female candidates are exempted from the application fee.

What is the minimum qualification required for RRC Prayagraj Apprentice posts?

Candidates need a Class 10 pass certificate with minimum 50% marks along with an ITI/NCVT certificate in a related trade.

Is there a written exam for this apprentice recruitment?

No, selection is based on a merit list prepared from Class 10 and ITI marks, followed by document verification.

What is the age limit for RRC Prayagraj Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must be between 15 and 24 years of age as on 07 July 2026, with age relaxation as per rules.

Where can candidates check the trade-wise and division-wise vacancy breakup?

The complete trade-wise and division-wise vacancy details are provided in the official notification released by RRC Prayagraj.
