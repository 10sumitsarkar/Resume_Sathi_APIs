<h2>Selection Process</h2>
<p>There's no interview at any stage of this recruitment - selection runs purely on your Prelims and Mains scores, followed by document verification. One thing that trips up a lot of first-time applicants: your Prelims marks don't carry forward into the final merit list. Prelims only decides who gets shortlisted for Mains; the Mains score alone determines your final rank.</p>
<ul>
<li>Preliminary Examination - 100 Questions / 100 Marks / 60 Minutes, Split Into English (30Q), Numerical Ability (35Q) and Reasoning Ability (35Q), Each With a 20-Minute Sectional Time Limit</li>
<li>Main Examination - 160 Questions / 200 Marks / 125 Minutes, Covering General/Financial Awareness (40Q/50M), General English (40Q/40M), Reasoning Ability (40Q/60M) and Quantitative Aptitude (40Q/50M)</li>
<li>Document Verification for Candidates Provisionally Allotted a Bank</li>
</ul>
<h2>Pay Scale</h2>
<p>Basic pay for the Customer Service Associate post starts at Rs. 24,050 and progresses to Rs. 64,480 over the course of increments. With allowances added in, the starting gross salary typically works out to around Rs. 40,000-42,000 a month, though this varies slightly by posting location.</p>
<h2>How to Fill IBPS Clerk CRP CSA-XVI Online Form 2026</h2>
<ul>
<li>Visit ibps.in, Open the CRP CSA-XVI Section and Click "New Registration" to Generate Your Provisional Registration Number and Password.</li>
<li>Upload Your Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression, Handwritten Declaration and 10th/SSLC Certificate as per the Format Specified in Annexure IV of the Notification.</li>
<li>Fill the Form Section by Section Using "Save and Next," Since Saving Partway Through Lets You Come Back Without Losing Progress.</li>
<li>Pay the Fee Within the Same 01-21 August Window - a Saved but Unpaid Form Is Treated as an Invalid Application.</li>
<li>Double-Check That Your Payment Actually Went Through Before Closing the Tab - This Is a Common Point Where Applications Get Missed.</li>
<li>Print the Final Application Form and the Payment Receipt for Your Records.</li>
</ul>

<h2>IBPS Clerk CRP CSA-XVI Preparation Tips 2026</h2>
<ul>
<li>Since Prelims Marks Don't Count Toward the Final Merit, Treat It as a Speed Test - Focus on Clearing Sectional Cutoffs Comfortably Rather Than Maximizing Your Score There.</li>
<li>Mains Is Where the Real Battle Happens - Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning Together Carry 110 of the 200 Marks, So Prioritize Them in Your Daily Practice.</li>
<li>For General/Financial Awareness, Track Recent RBI Policy Changes, Repo Rate Movements and Major Banking Sector News From the Last 4-6 Months.</li>
<li>Work on Speed in English - With Only 35 Minutes for 40 Questions in Mains, Reading Comprehension Passages Need to Be Tackled Efficiently, Not Read Word by Word.</li>
<li>Practice Local Language Basics if It Isn't Your Mother Tongue, Since Proficiency in the State/UT's Local Language Is a Stated Eligibility Requirement.</li>
</ul>
